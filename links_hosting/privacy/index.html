Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Our Privacy Commitment

KinCircle is built on a simple principle: family safety software should bring calm and peace of mind without compromising your privacy. We collect only the data necessary to provide circle location sharing, safe zone arrival alerts, and emergency crash detection. We never sell your personal data, never track you for third-party advertisements, and never share location information outside your accepted family circles.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you register or sign in, we collect your email address and display name.
  • Real-Time Location Data: With your explicit consent, we collect precise GPS latitude and longitude coordinates to share with members of your active family circle and trigger geofence safe zone alerts.
  • Family Circles & Membership: Information about family circles you create or join, member lists, and invite tokens.
  • Motion & Sensor Data: Accelerometer and motion sensor telemetry collected during active trips to detect severe impact (crash detection) and calculate driving safety scores.

3. How Your Data Is Shared

Within Your Circles: Your real-time location and safe zone status are visible only to accepted members of your family circle. You can toggle Invisible Mode at any time to temporarily hide your location.

No Third-Party Advertising: We do not broker, rent, or sell location or behavioral data to data brokers or ad networks.

4. Backend Infrastructure & Service Providers

We rely on trusted cloud infrastructure to operate KinCircle securely:

  • Google Cloud & Firebase: Used for secure authentication, Cloud Firestore database storage, Cloud Functions, and Firebase Hosting.
  • SendGrid (Twilio): Used strictly for transmitting transactional family circle invitation emails requested by users.
  • Analytics & Cookies: We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) with anonymized IP tracking solely on our public marketing and landing web pages to measure aggregate website traffic. Analytics tracking is not present on private dashboards or authenticated family data pages.

5. Data Security & Encryption

All data in transit is encrypted using industry-standard TLS. In accordance with GDPR and data portability standards, full account data exports requested by users can be locally encrypted on device using AES-256 with secure enclave key storage.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have full control over your personal information:

  • Leave or delete family circles at any time.
  • Export all stored account data and location history.
  • Permanently delete your KinCircle account and associated data directly from the app or by contacting support.
