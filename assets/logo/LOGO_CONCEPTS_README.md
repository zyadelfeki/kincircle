# KinCircle Logo Concepts

## Concept 1: The Connected Rings
**File:** logo_concept_1_rings.png
**Description:** Two interlocking rings in modern gradient colors (blue #2196F3 and green #4CAF50). The rings represent family connection and unity. Clean, minimalist design with subtle shadows for depth.

**Design Specifications:**
- Size: 1024x1024 px
- Background: Transparent
- Primary Ring: Blue gradient (#1976D2 to #2196F3)
- Secondary Ring: Green gradient (#388E3C to #4CAF50)
- Ring thickness: 80px
- Interlocking at 45-degree angle

## Concept 2: The Stylized Pin
**File:** logo_concept_2_pin.png
**Description:** A modern map pin with an integrated heart symbol in the center. Combines location tracking with family love and care.

**Design Specifications:**
- Size: 1024x1024 px
- Background: Transparent
- Pin color: Warm orange-red gradient (#FF5722 to #FF7043)
- Heart: White with subtle shadow
- Pin shadow: Soft gray below

## Concept 3: The Abstract 'K'
**File:** logo_concept_3_k.png
**Description:** A geometric interpretation of the letter 'K' formed by connected nodes and lines, suggesting a network or family tree structure.

**Design Specifications:**
- Size: 1024x1024 px
- Background: Transparent
- Primary color: Deep navy blue (#1A237E)
- Accent color: Bright cyan (#00BCD4)
- Geometric shapes with connecting lines
- Modern, tech-focused aesthetic
