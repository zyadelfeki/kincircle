## Announcement Blog Post (≈300 words)

### Introducing Smart Alerts: A New Level of Peace of Mind

Since day one, KinCircle's mission has been simple: help families feel connected and safe without all the digital noise. Today we're taking a huge step forward with **Smart Alerts**, an AI-powered feature that quietly looks out for your loved ones and taps you only when something seems unusual.

For the past few months we've been training a privacy-preserving machine-learning model on anonymous location patterns (never names or emails!). Instead of blasting you with every movement, Smart Alerts learns routines—like when kids normally arrive at school or when a partner heads home from work—and flags only the moments that fall outside those patterns.

During our closed beta we hit **85 % precision**, saving parents roughly *200 distracting pings per week*. The best part? Everything runs on secure Google Cloud infrastructure, and your raw location history never leaves our private project.

Getting started is easy:

1. Update the app to the latest version.  
2. Open **Settings → Smart Features**.  
3. Tap **Enable Smart Alerts** and give consent.  

That's it—KinCircle will take it from there, learning in the background and letting you know when something deserves your attention. We're incredibly excited about the peace of mind this will bring to families everywhere and can't wait to hear what you think.

Stay safe and stay connected,  
**The KinCircle Team**
