# KinCircle FAQ

This FAQ powers in‑app Help and the support chatbot. Keep answers short, actionable, and link to settings/screens when possible.

## Account and Sign‑In

- How do I sign in? Use your email or Google/Apple account on the Welcome screen. If you see an error, check your network and try again.
- I changed phones. Will my data sync? Yes. Sign in with the same account; your cloud data (profiles, summaries, scores) will sync. Local incident history is device‑only by design.

## Family and Invites

- How do I invite family members? Share your invite link from Settings → Family. The recipient installs the app and signs in.
- The invite link didn’t work. What now? Make sure the app is installed before opening the link. If it still fails, open the app → Settings → Family → Enter Code and paste the referral/invite code.

## Driver Safety

- What is the Driver Safety Score? A 0–100 score updated weekly from anonymized summaries (counts of incidents) uploaded by your device.
- What events are detected? On‑device detection of harsh braking and rapid acceleration using phone sensors. Raw data never leaves the device.
- How do I start/stop Driving Mode? Open Driving Mode from the Home screen. Tap Start Drive to begin and End Drive to stop.
- Can I force a weekly summary upload? In debug builds, Safety Report shows an Upload Summary Now button. Otherwise, uploads occur automatically once per week.

## Smart Alerts

- What are Smart Alerts? Optional AI‑assisted alerts about meaningful changes or risks. Configure from Settings → Alerts.
- How do I turn alerts on/off? Go to Settings → Alerts and toggle the alert types you want.

## Privacy and Data

- Do you track my location all the time? Only with your consent and only for features you enable. Driver Safety uses on‑device sensors and stores incidents locally.
- What data is uploaded? Weekly counts of incident types and timestamps. No raw sensor streams.
- How do I delete my data? Go to Settings → Privacy → Delete Account/Data. Deletion is permanent.

## Troubleshooting

- Battery drain? Reduce update frequency in Settings → Battery, disable non‑critical alerts, and ensure the app isn’t restricted by system battery savers.
- Sensors not detecting? Confirm permissions in Settings → Permissions. Restart Driving Mode. Reboot the phone if needed.
- Score isn’t updating? Scores update weekly. Ensure you’re signed in and connected to the internet.

## Billing

- How do trials and Premium work? Trials unlock Premium features for a limited time. After the trial, you can subscribe in Settings → Billing.
- Refunds? See Settings → Billing → Refund Policy. For store purchases, follow Google Play/App Store refund processes.

## Contact Support

- In the app, go to Settings → Help → Contact Support. Provide details (device model, OS version, app version, steps). The bot will try to help first and escalate if needed.
