# KinCircle Support Email Templates

Use these professionally-worded, empathetic templates when replying to users. Replace bracketed placeholders (e.g. **[User Name]**) before sending.

---

### 1. Responding to a Bug Report

Subject: We're on it – thanks for reporting this issue 🛠️

Hi **[User Name]**,  

Thank you for taking the time to let us know about the problem you encountered with KinCircle. I'm sorry for the disruption this has caused.

I've shared your report with our engineering team and we're already investigating. To help us resolve this as quickly as possible, could you please reply with any of the following that you're comfortable sharing?

• A brief description of what you were doing when the issue occurred.  
• The steps you took just before the problem appeared.  
• The device model and OS version you're using.  

In the meantime, restarting the app (or your device) can sometimes provide a temporary fix.

We'll keep you updated on our progress and will let you know as soon as a fix is available. Thank you again for helping us make KinCircle better. If there's anything else we can do for you, just hit reply.

Warm regards,  
**[Your Name]**  
KinCircle Support
