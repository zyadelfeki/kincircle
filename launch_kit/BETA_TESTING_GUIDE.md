# Welcome to the KinCircle AI Beta Test!

Thank you for helping us build the future of family safety. Your feedback is crucial.

### Your Mission:

Please use the app with your family for 3-5 days and try to complete the following tasks.

**Setup:**
- [ ] Successfully create an account and set up your profile.
- [ ] Create a family and invite at least one other family member.
- [ ] Ensure your family member can successfully accept the invite and join.

**Core Features:**
- [ ] Verify that you can see all family members correctly on the map.
- [ ] Create a Geofence for "Home" and another for "School" or "Work".
- [ ] Test the **Smart Alerts** feature. Did you receive any alerts? Were they helpful?
- [ ] Test the **SOS button**. Did your family members receive the high-priority notification?
- [ ] Test the **Invisible Mode** toggle. Did your marker correctly disappear from your family's map?

**Feedback:**
Please report any bugs, crashes, or confusing experiences. A link to our feedback survey will be sent via email.
